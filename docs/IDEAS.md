# Ideas parked for later

Ideas we've talked through and decided to keep, but not build yet. Each has
the reasoning so far, so it can be picked up without starting over.

## Mimic, a wild card monster (parked)

- No element: charges from **every** match, at about a third of the usual rate.
- **Echo**: its move copies the last move the rival fired.
- A counter-pick: strong against big ultimates, weak against chip damage.
  Interesting in Draft and Random.
- Art: 3 stages + idles through PixelLab, about 30-40 generations.
- Watch for: Echo copying a seal or a blast should resolve as the Mimic's
  own cast (its own element for blasts, its own side for heals).

## A dark element (skipped for now)

A sixth gem still leaves a working board (it almost never runs out of moves),
but every element charges about a third slower. Simulated, 7x7, greedy player:

| Board | Matching swaps per board | One element's gems per move | Lines of 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 gems, 7x7 (now) | 11.3 | 1.40 | 31% |
| 6 gems, 7x7 | 8.4 | 0.88 | 26% |
| 6 gems, 8x8 | 11.7 | 0.96 | 31% |

So it needs either charge costs about a third lower or an 8x8 board (smaller
tiles on phones), plus four new monster lines and a rebalance. A cheaper
version: "Shade" gems as a board hazard (one tile, no monsters).
